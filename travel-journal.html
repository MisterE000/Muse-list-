SHARED TRAVEL JOURNAL

Mr.E and Mrs.E — RV trips and other travels, in Witherwings and beyond.

A place for both of you to keep the trips, as they happen — where you went, what you saw, what made you laugh. One entry per trip. No rules, no grades. Just the road, remembered.

How to use this journal

  • Add a line to the Trip Index for every new trip, so you can find it fast.
  • Copy the Entry Template for each new trip — same fields every time: dates, location, notes, highlights, photos.
  • Use the Reusable Trip Preparation List for every trip: check items off, then start fresh next time.
  • Write a little each evening if you can. Two honest lines that night beat two polished pages a month later.
  • Either of you can add to an entry. It is shared on purpose.

Reusable trip preparation list

Use this same list for every trip. Check off each item as you go. Add your own items in the blank rows at the end — if you need it twice, it belongs on the list.

A week or more before you leave

  • Confirm dates, destination, and any launch / event dates — and note that SpaceX dates often slip
  • Book campgrounds / parks you need (for a launch: Isla Blanca Park for launch day, as few days as cost allows)
  • Sketch the route: highways if a launch is imminent, non-highways preferred when it is not
  • Mark dump + fresh-water stops every 3rd or 4th day along the route
  • Mark Twice The Ice locations spaced 3–4 days apart for drinking water and ice
  • Check overnight plan: boondocking / rest areas / Walmart are fine; do not arrive in a forest after dark unless you scoped it in daylight
  • If overnight lows will be over 80°F, plan a campground for those nights; if a heavy storm is forecast, plan a Walmart stop for Wi-Fi
  • Hold / pause mail and deliveries, and tell anyone who needs to know you will be away

Witherwings — RV mechanical and safety

  • Oil change if due — Witherwings (E350, 7.3L V8): 5W-30, 9 qt, filter Mobil M1-210. For a launch trip, finish it at least 3 days before the actual launch date
  • Check all fluids, belts, hoses, and tire pressures (including spare); look for leaks underneath
  • Check lights, brakes, wipers, mirrors, and dash cam (USB-C lead packed)
  • Fuel up, and check propane level
  • Test generator / house systems, lights inside, water pump, and refrigerator on both power sources
  • Charge / check the lithium house bank — 920 Ah; note voltage (13.3V is about 70%, 13.1V is about 20%)
  • Close and latch every cargo bay door; check door, window, and toilet seals if resealing is due
  • Tools, spare fuses, flashlight / work light, and roadside kit on board

Water, tanks, and utilities

  • Dump black and gray tanks, rinse, and add tank treatment before you leave
  • Fill the fresh tank; check the additional fresh-tank space / tank if fitted
  • Remember: we do not drink the RV water — stock bottled water and plan Twice The Ice stops
  • If catching rainwater: separate rain tank clean and ready, filter ready — never straight to drinking use
  • Sewer hose, gloves, fresh-water hose, and water filter / pressure regulator packed and stowed
  • Check water pump switch, hot-water system, and shower / taps for leaks

Power, Starlink, and connectivity

  • Starlink (permanently mounted) — power it up and confirm it connects before you leave
  • Shore-power cord, adapters, and surge protection packed
  • Charge phones, laptops, and battery packs; pack all charging cables, including USB-C
  • Download offline maps, launch / weather apps, and iOverlander spots for the route while you have good signal
  • Mrs.E work setup checked if traveling on a workday (Mon–Fri) — laptop, charger, and Starlink signal

Groceries and kitchen

  • Build the shared grocery list by store and shop it
  • Stock coffee and morning supplies — coffee first thing is part of the routine
  • Fridge staples, easy first-night meal, snacks, and bottled drinking water loaded
  • Twice The Ice plan for the first leg: where is the first stop, 3–4 days out?
  • Kitchen basics checked: propane / stove, pans, utensils, paper towels, trash bags, dish soap
  • Empty home fridge of anything that will spoil while you are away

Documents, money, and phones

  • Driver's licenses, registration, insurance, and roadside-assistance cards in the RV
  • Campground / park confirmations, launch viewing info, and addresses saved on the phone and on paper
  • Cards and cash; notify bank / cards of travel if needed
  • Medications, glasses, and first-aid kit packed
  • Phone list updated: family, campground, and emergency contacts
  • This journal — and a pen — packed where you will actually use it

Clothing, personal, and home — before you lock up

  • Clothing for heat, cool nights, and rain; hats, sunscreen, and bug spray
  • Toiletries, towels, bedding, and laundry bag
  • Shoes for walking, camp, and wet ground
  • At home: windows and doors locked, thermostat set, lights / timers set, trash out (Thursday night — check if it is a recycle B week)
  • Plants / pets / deck / anything a neighbor or family member is watching — handed off with a note

Day of departure — final walk-around

  • All cargo bay doors closed and latched; steps up, antenna / Starlink clear, cords and hoses stowed
  • Refrigerator latched, loose items stowed, cupboards latched
  • Final check inside the house, then lock up
  • Record starting mileage, fuel, fresh / black / gray levels, and house-battery voltage in this trip's journal entry
  • First stop and tonight's stop entered in navigation — before you roll

On the road — every 3 to 4 days

  • Dump black and gray tanks; fill fresh water
  • Stop at Twice The Ice for drinking water and ice
  • Top up groceries, fuel, and propane as needed
  • Check tires, lights, battery voltage, and anything rattling or leaking
  • Write two lines in the journal that evening: where are we, and what was the highlight?

When you get home

  • Dump and rinse tanks, drain / store hoses, and turn off the water pump
  • Empty the fridge, prop the fridge door if Witherwings will sit, and remove trash and laundry
  • Finish the journal entry: highlights, best moment, and tips for next time
  • Add anything you wished you had packed to this master list
