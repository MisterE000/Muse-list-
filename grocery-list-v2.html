Shared version: check an item and it drops to the bottom of its store list, and the list re-sorts alphabetically. Use ↑ ↓ to nudge an item, ✕ to remove it, and the boxes under each store to add new items. New items show in green at the top of their store until you press that store's Reset list button after shopping — reset un-checks everything for next time and folds the green items into the list in normal color. Every change saves to the shared list. First time on a phone, tap Connect this phone and paste that phone's list token once — it is stored only on that phone, never in this page.

How this list works

  • → Check the box when an item is bought — it moves to the bottom and the list re-sorts A–Z.
  • → ↑ and ↓ move an item one step; that order holds until the next check or uncheck re-sorts the list.
  • → ✕ removes an item. The add boxes under each store add new items.
  • → Newly added items are green and at the top of their store, so fresh additions stand out.
  • → After a shopping trip, press the store's Reset list button: everything un-checks ready for next time, and the green items turn normal color and take their normal place in the list.
  • → Changes save to the shared list within a few seconds. Reopen or return to the page to pick up changes from the other phone.

Shared list data lives with the shared lists site (grocery-data.json). The original static list is still at grocery-list.html. Page updated November 4, 2026 (per-store Reset buttons + green new items).
