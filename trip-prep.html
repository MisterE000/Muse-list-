Shared checklists: work through each section and check items off as they are done — checked items drop to the bottom of their section. Items stay in the order you put them (no alphabetical sorting). Use ↑ ↓ to nudge an item, ✕ to remove it, and the boxes under each section to add new items. New items show in green at the top of their section until you press that section’s Reset list button — after a trip, reset un-checks everything for next time and folds the green items into the list in normal color. Every change saves to the shared list. This page uses the same phone connection as the grocery list, so a phone already connected there is connected here too.

How this list works

  • → Sections start in trip order: Pre-trip → Preparing → Day-of-departure → Walk-out → In-transit → After returning, plus Winterizing for the off-season.
  • → Rearrange sections as the trip unfolds: ⤒ beside a section title sends that section straight to the top (Preparing while you pack, In-transit once you are rolling), and ↑ ↓ nudge it one step. The order is shared, so both phones show the same arrangement.
  • → Check the box when an item is done — it moves to the bottom of its section. Nothing sorts alphabetically: items stay in the order you arrange them.
  • → ↑ and ↓ move an item one step; ✕ removes an item. The add boxes under each section add new items.
  • → Newly added items are green and at the top of their section, so fresh additions stand out.
  • → When a section is finished with (or the trip is over), press its Reset list button: everything un-checks ready for next time, and the green items turn normal color and take their normal place in the list.
  • → Changes save to the shared list within a few seconds. Reopen or return to the page to pick up changes from the other phone.
